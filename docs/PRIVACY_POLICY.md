# Privacy Policy for aham-vision

Effective date: August 14, 2026

aham-vision performs real-time object detection entirely on the user's Android device. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, does not connect to a server, and does not sell or share personal data.

## Camera and microphone

Camera access is required to provide live video preview, offline object detection, and optional video recording. Microphone access is optional and is used only to add audio when the user starts a recording. Camera frames and microphone audio are processed locally and are not transmitted by the app.

## Recordings

Recordings are created only when the user taps the Record button. They are stored in the app-specific Movies directory on the device. The app does not upload or share recordings. Users can remove recordings using Android file management tools or by clearing/uninstalling the app.

## Data retention and security

The app itself does not collect or retain personal data. Locally created recordings remain under the user's control. Android application sandboxing protects app-specific files.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose computer-vision utility and is not directed specifically to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.
