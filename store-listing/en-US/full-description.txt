See the world through fast, private, on-device computer vision.

aham-vision uses an embedded YOLO model to detect people, birds, animals, vehicles, and everyday objects in a live camera view. Detection works without an internet connection, and camera frames are never sent to a server.

Features:
• Real-time object detection with labels and confidence scores
• Detection across the standard 80 COCO object categories
• Video recording while live detection continues
• Optional audio recording
• Fully offline model inference
• No account, advertising, analytics, or cloud upload

Performance depends on your device's camera and processor. Recordings remain in the app-specific Movies directory on your device.
